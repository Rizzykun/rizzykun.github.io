Toolbox

The tools and references I actually reach for during a CTF.

1 minute read

A running list of what I use. If a writeup on this site mentions a tool, it is probably here.

CC BY-NC-SA 4.0

CyberChef

The cyber swiss army knife — base64, XOR, magic decode. Used in nearly every writeup here.

FTK Imager

Mounting and carving disk images. How the Dumpster Diving flag came out of the Recycle Bin.

Wayback Machine

Archived snapshots of dead sites. Essential when the OSINT trail runs through the 2000s web.

Wireshark

Packet analysis — spotting the C2-style HTTP beacons in a PCAP.

Autopsy

Open-source digital forensics platform, for when FTK Imager is not enough.

ExifTool

Metadata in and out of images. First stop on any geolocation challenge.

Google Advanced Search Operators

Dorking reference. Most OSINT challenges are won or lost on query construction.

CTFtime

Event calendar, team rankings and the archive of everyone else's writeups.
