Agentic AI-Driven CTF 2026

Aug 2026 * · 6th place

Entered mid-CPTS-prep to test whether a learned pentest methodology transfers to unfamiliar, AI-themed challenges. Takeaway: AI accelerates idea generation and analysis but its output still needs validating, and returning to a structured methodology beats blind tool-switching when stuck.

NEXSEC 2025 — Intervarsity Cyber Forensic Challenge

Dec 2025 * · 3rd place · RM1,000

Qualified through a jeopardy-format round into a finals round built like real incident response: raw artifacts, full analysis, and a written incident report, with only one hour on day two to answer from prior analysis. Handled the forensics category for the team; prepared with HTB Sherlock challenges and weekly write-ups.

Writing

Short pieces I put on LinkedIn rather than here:

  • AI is an accelerator, not a replacement for reasoning (Aug 2026 *) — Reflection from the UCSI Agentic AI CTF on validating AI output and falling back on methodology when stuck.
  • Bad documentation, not difficulty, is what slows you down (Jul 2026 *) — On restructuring security notes: separating concepts from commands, linking notes instead of nesting folders, and writing for your future self.
