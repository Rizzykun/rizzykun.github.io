I'm **Rizzykun**. I play Capture The Flag competitions and write up the
challenges I solve — mostly **OSINT** and **digital forensics**, with the
occasional web challenge when I'm feeling brave.

## What's on this site

Every post here is a challenge I actually solved, written the way I worked
through it: the dead ends included. I'm not writing polished tutorials — I'm
keeping notes I can come back to, and that a beginner might find useful because
they show the messy middle rather than just the answer.

## Competitions

- **SUN CTF 2025** — forensics and OSINT
- **Siber Siaga 2025** — preliminary and finale
- **Securinets CTF 2025** — preliminary
- **WHY CTF 2025** — web
