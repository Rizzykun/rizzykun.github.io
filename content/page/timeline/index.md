**Sunway CTF 2026** — Team CTF; a return to competitive play after a stretch focused on CPTS preparation.

**Agentic AI-Driven CTF 2026** — Entered mid-CPTS-prep to test whether a learned pentest methodology transfers to unfamiliar, AI-themed challenges. Takeaway: AI accelerates idea generation and analysis but its output still needs validating, and returning to a structured methodology beats blind tool-switching when stuck.

**Taming iCTF F8 — Grand Finale** — Short, high-pressure final round under four hours. Exposed Active Directory as the next area to build depth in.

**iGOH 2025 — Finals** — One challenge short of solving everything in the prelims.

**NEXSEC 2025 — Intervarsity Cyber Forensic Challenge** — Qualified through a jeopardy-format round into a finals round built like real incident response: raw artifacts, full analysis, and a written incident report, with only one hour on day two to answer from prior analysis. Handled the forensics category for the team; prepared with HTB Sherlock challenges and weekly write-ups.

**SherpaCTF 2025** — Individual offline event, joined primarily for networking rather than placing.

**IBOH 2025 — International Battle of Hackers** — Stage 1 was a jeopardy round that qualified the team for Stage 2, a first-time Attack & Defence format where the opening hours go to patching your own machine before other teams exploit it.

**System Override CTF** — On-site event with roughly 200 participants.

**Siber Siaga 2025 CODE COMBAT** — First time the team reached a CTF final. One challenge away from the top-5 podium.

**SunCTF 2025** — Team CTF focused on collaboration and problem-solving under time pressure.

**L3AK CTF 2025** — 48-hour online competition joined during semester break.

**ICECTF** — Placed top 10 as a two-person team, against a field where most top-10 teams had three members.

**UMCS CTF 2025** — Solved two challenges; just outside the top-25 cutoff for the final round.
